Frequently Asked

Common questions from owners and contractors

Can Nexcamp take over an existing camp mid-contract?

Yes. Our 45-day mobilisation plan is designed for both new and existing camps: site survey and handover checklist in week one, team onboarding and service mapping by day 20, and full operating routines with a KPI baseline by day 45.

Do you offer individual services or only full camp management?

Both. Many clients engage us for the full operating model, but we also deliver single service lines such as housekeeping and laundry, catering oversight, maintenance and MEP, or HSE support.

How do you report performance?

Clients receive a Monthly Management Pack covering occupancy, maintenance register, HSE observations, service-level performance, resident feedback and open decisions, supported by daily and weekly operational logs.

Which regions do you cover?

We are based in Abu Dhabi and support sites across the United Arab Emirates, including remote and industrial locations.
