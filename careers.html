Nexcamp is growing its site teams across the UAE. If you take pride in safe, clean and well-run places to live, we would like to hear from you.

Why Nexcamp

A workplace built on safety, respect, reliability, accountability and excellence

Our people are the service. We invest in training, clear procedures and role-specific uniforms and equipment so every team member can do their job well and be recognised for it.

  • Structured induction and HSE training for every role
  • Clear career paths from site roles to supervisory and management positions
  • Respectful, multicultural teams operating to consistent standards
  • Professional uniforms and PPE provided
Roles We Hire

Opportunities across every part of camp operations

We regularly recruit for the roles below. Submit a general application and we will contact you when a suitable position opens.

Management

Camp managers and account leads responsible for site performance and client reporting.

Administration

Occupancy records, resident onboarding, documentation and authority coordination.

Supervisors

Shift and service supervisors coordinating daily routines and quality checks.

Housekeeping

Room attendants, common-area cleaners and laundry operators.

Maintenance

HVAC, electrical and plumbing technicians and multi-skilled maintenance staff.

HSE

Safety officers conducting inspections, inductions and emergency readiness.

Camp Patrol & Security

Gate control, patrols, visitor management and incident reporting.

Catering

Chefs, cooks, kitchen stewards and mess-hall service staff.
