Management Approach

Mobilise → Stabilise → Control → Improve

A disciplined management cycle creates confidence from day one and drives measurable improvement over time.

1

Mobilise

Site survey, handover plan, team deployment and baseline condition review.

2

Stabilise

Daily routines, resident coordination, housekeeping standards and maintenance priorities.

3

Control

Reporting cadence, KPI tracking, cost visibility and authority coordination.

4

Improve

Corrective actions, service upgrades and resident-experience enhancement.

Mobilisation Plan

A clear 45-day transition from award to steady-state operation

A disciplined start-up process creates confidence from day one.

Days 1–7

Kick-off, site survey, document review, handover checklist and mobilisation governance.

Days 8–20

Team onboarding, accommodation zoning, service mapping and client reporting templates.

Days 21–35

Operating routines begin: housekeeping, maintenance logs, security coordination and resident communication.

Days 36–45

Performance baseline, corrective-action plan, KPI dashboard and steady-state operation.
